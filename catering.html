Catering

let us feed your next gig

Blow everyone away at your next gig

Big or small, we can work with what you need and get our delicious food delivered to you hot, anywhere in Auckland.

We're expert at a variety of plant-based cuisines — just let us know what you want and let us work our magic. If you like, we can also bring our warm and friendly team, and eco-friendly tableware, to serve out the meal.

Working with us is easy and fuss-free — so at least you don't have to stress about the food while you're busy getting your event together.
