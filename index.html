More than a food kiosk

We're a not-for-profit, here to help students live a healthy, conscious and sustainable life. Our food is tasty and wholesome — it relaxes the mind, fills the belly, and satisfies the heart and soul.

  • Tasty & diet friendly

    A revolving menu across a variety of cuisines, with gluten-free options.

  • Students b4 profits

    Nutrient rich, affordable and satisfying meals that keep students healthy.

  • Student wellness

    We support well-being activities on campus.

  • Sustainable

    Plant-based food, made with seasonal produce and distributed sustainably.
