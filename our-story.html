Our story

Who are we?

We're a not-for-profit, and our reason for being is to help improve the well-being of the student community — empowering students to live a healthy, conscious and sustainable life.

We do that by bringing fresh, tasty and affordable food to campus. We also support well-being and sustainability initiatives around the university.

Why Conscious Eats?

Student life generally means long days packed with classes, assessments and a job on the side to pay the bills. With so much going on, health takes a back seat. Diet gets neglected — which affects sleep, mood and digestion, leaving you overwhelmed and flat. That hits productivity, which hits mental health, and round it goes.

We want to break that chain reaction. For the price of a coffee or a burger you can have a tasty, hearty, balanced meal that gives you the nutrition you need to power on.

Why are we called Conscious Eats?

Because our food is made with the highest intentions. From cooking to serving, everything is done consciously and with gratitude, acknowledging the ultimate source.

Enjoy your plate of tasty, karma free food — supercharged with happy vibes.
