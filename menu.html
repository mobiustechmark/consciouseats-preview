Everything is plant-based

Our menu rotates across a variety of cuisines, and there are usually gluten free options on too. If you've got an allergy or something you need to avoid, just ask us at the kiosk — we're happy to talk you through what's in today's dishes.

We use no nuts, dairy, onion or garlic. All our food is onion and garlic free. Just lots of love.
