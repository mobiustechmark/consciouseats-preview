Our catering menu

Three set-meal packages, all plant-based. Pick a package below and choose your curries, then add any extras — the estimated total updates as you go.

Basic Meal

$15 per person

  • Rice (plain, cumin or turmeric)
  • 1 curry from List One
  • Vegetable nuggets (2)
  • Poppadom (2)
  • Green salad with dressing
  • Dessert — fruit pudding

Full Meal

Popular

$20 per person

  • Rice (plain, cumin or turmeric)
  • 2 curries — one from List One + one from List Two
  • Vegetable fritters (2)
  • Poppadom (2)
  • Green salad with dressing
  • Dessert — fruit pudding

Superior Meal

$25 per person

  • Entrée — dhal or sambhar
  • Special rice (royal or spinach)
  • 2 curries from List One and/or List Two
  • Vegetable fritters (3)
  • Poppadom (3)
  • Fresh green salad, sunflower-seed dressing
  • Dessert — fruit pudding with sweet custard

Curry — List One

  • Creamy chickpea
  • Mexican chilli beans
  • Not-butter Not-chicken (jackfruit)
  • Malaysian curry
  • Veggie korma
  • Satay curry (peanuts or sunflower)
  • Thai green curry (tofu)

Curry — List Two

  • Thai Massaman (tofu & veg)
  • Indian spiced potato & spinach
  • Creamy spiced spinach tofu
  • Chinese sweet & sour (walnut dumplings)
  • Manchurian (cauliflower sweet & sour)

List Two is available on the Full and Superior packages.
